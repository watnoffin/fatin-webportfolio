## The Problem

University career offices and programme directors lack a systematic, data-driven view of how graduates are faring in the job market — and whether satisfaction with their degree correlates with employment outcomes. GradTrack provides that view.

## My Role

Sole researcher and developer for my Final Year Project. Designed the survey instrument, collected and cleaned the data, built the analytical pipeline, and delivered the Power BI dashboard.

## Tools & Technologies

`Python (Pandas, NumPy)` · `Power BI` · `Statistical Analysis` · `Survey Design` · `Data Cleaning`

## Approach

### Survey Design

The survey captured both factual employment data (status, time-to-first-job, sector) and satisfaction metrics across curriculum relevance, career support, and overall experience — enabling correlation analysis between programme quality perceptions and outcomes.

### Cleaning & Preparation

Python and Pandas handled the full cleaning pipeline: standardising free-text categorical entries, applying median imputation for numeric fields with low missingness, and flagging records with excessive missing data for exclusion.

### Statistical Analysis

Satisfaction metrics were scored on a composite Likert scale. Correlation analysis identified which satisfaction dimensions most strongly associate with employment outcomes. Group comparisons tested whether differences across programmes were statistically significant.

### Predictive Modelling

A binary classification model (employed vs. not employed within six months) was trained on GPA, satisfaction scores, and field-of-study features. Feature importance analysis surfaced which inputs were most predictive.

### Power BI Dashboard

The final deliverable was an interactive Power BI report allowing programme directors to drill into employment rates, satisfaction breakdowns, and predictive scores by cohort — turning the analysis into an actionable planning tool.
