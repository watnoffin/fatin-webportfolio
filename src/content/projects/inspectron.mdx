## The Problem

Home inspections are time-consuming, expensive, and dependent on the availability of qualified inspectors. Property buyers and landlords often defer them — and when defects go undetected, small problems compound into costly repairs. INSPECTRON makes a first-pass inspection accessible from a smartphone photo.

## My Role

Team member (Champion team). Contributed to defect detection pipeline design, API integration with Gemini and IBM watsonx.ai, and report generation logic.

## Tools & Technologies

`Gemini API` · `IBM watsonx.ai` · `Python` · `Computer Vision` · `Prompt Engineering`

## Approach

### Defect Detection

Gemini's vision capability is given a structured prompt instructing it to identify specific defect categories (cracks, mold, water damage, structural anomalies) and return a JSON list of findings with location descriptions. This structured output feeds directly into the next stage without manual parsing.

### Severity Classification

A deterministic mapping layer converts Gemini's defect type labels into severity levels (Low → Critical), ensuring consistent classification independent of LLM variability.

### Report Generation with watsonx.ai

IBM watsonx.ai receives the structured defect list and generates a professional-tone inspection report. The prompt is templated to produce:
- An executive summary of overall property condition.
- A per-defect section with severity, description, and recommended repair action.
- A priority list for urgent attention.

## Outcome

Champion at **Bridgehack to Industry (UiTM × IBM, September–October 2024)**. The prototype demonstrated that a two-API pipeline (vision + language model) could produce inspection reports comparable in structure and depth to a professional report, from a single photo upload.
